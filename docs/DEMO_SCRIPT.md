# Msingi — 2-Minute Judge Walkthrough Script ⏱️

**Target Duration**: 120 seconds  
**Speaker Tone**: Confident, energetic, culturally grounded, technically precise.  
**Audience**: Hackathon Judges (#1MillionDevs Vibe Coding Hackathon).

---

### [0:00 - 0:20] The Hook: The Cloud Learning Reality in Africa
> *"Habari judges! Across Africa, from Nairobi to Lagos and Kigali, millions of young developers are eager to validate their skills with the Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900) certification.*
>
> *Yet, they face three major roadblocks: expensive data for video streams, abstract Western analogies like snowplows, and predatory exam dumps.*
>
> *Enter **Msingi**—the Swahili word for 'Foundation'. An offline-first, mobile-first PWA that makes Azure concepts crystal clear through African analogies, backed by spaced repetition and recency-weighted readiness tracking."*

---

### [0:20 - 0:45] The 3-Step Setup & Baseline Calibration
> *(Screen: Onboarding Flow)*
>
> *"When a learner first arrives, Msingi sets their target exam date, weekly study hours, and preferred language—including Kiswahili, Sheng, or Nigerian Pidgin.*
>
> *A quick 10-question diagnostic quiz establishes their baseline mastery across all 3 official AZ-900 domains."*

---

### [0:45 - 1:10] The Dashboard & Smart Quiz Builder
> *(Screen: Home Dashboard & Quiz Page)*
>
> *"Here on the dashboard, learners see our **Recency-Weighted Readiness Score**. Unlike a simple average, Msingi weighs the last 50 attempts using a linear recency engine normalized to official domain weights, complete with a 15-skill topic heatmap.*
>
> *In the **Quiz Page**, learners can take a focused domain quiz, drill their weakest topics, or simulate the actual 45-minute, 40-question mock exam. Every question is 100% original, citing real African contexts like SACCO banking and M-Pesa throughput, with detailed explanations for why every distractor is wrong."*

---

### [1:10 - 1:30] Flashcards (SM-2) & Deterministic Planner
> *(Screen: Flashcards Page & Planner Page)*
>
> *"Missed questions are automatically transformed into flashcards powered by the **SuperMemo SM-2 algorithm**.*
>
> *Notice how every card features a memorable African analogy: for example, CapEx vs OpEx is explained as buying a noisy petrol generator upfront versus buying prepaid electricity tokens for your shop.*
>
> *Our **Deterministic Study Planner** maps out daily study tasks up to exam day and exports with one click to standard calendar apps via RFC 5545 `.ics`."*

---

### [1:30 - 1:50] Rafiki: The Grounded AI Study Buddy
> *(Screen: Rafiki Page)*
>
> *"Need instant help at 2 AM? Meet **Rafiki**—our contextual AI tutor. Rafiki can explain Azure Availability Zones using matatus and danfos in English, Kiswahili, Sheng, or Pidgin, complete with text-to-speech for hands-free learning.*
>
> *Best of all, Msingi works offline with zero API keys required, thanks to our local mock and storage architecture."*

---

### [1:50 - 2:00] Accuracy & Compliance Wrap-Up
> *(Screen: Accuracy Page & Footer)*
>
> *"Every single item in Msingi is mapped to the official July 20, 2026 Microsoft Learn outline with zero hallucinations and strict compliance notices.*
>
> *Msingi is more than an app—it is the foundation for one million African cloud careers. Asante sana!"*
